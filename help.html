Help & FAQ

The game crashes on launch. What do I do?
First, make sure you have at least 4 GB of RAM allocated to Minecraft in your launcher settings. If the crash persists, check the crash log (in the .minecraft/crash-reports/ folder) and post it in the Discord or on the Modrinth issues page.
Which mod loader does the modpack use?
The modpack currently targets [Forge / Fabric — TBD] for Minecraft 1.20.1. The modpack installer will handle this for you if you use the Modrinth App, CurseForge, or Prism Launcher.
Can I add more mods to the pack?
You can, but compatibility is not guaranteed. The modpack is designed as a curated experience; adding mods may break quests, alter world generation, or cause conflicts. Experiment on a separate profile.
Is multiplayer supported?
Yes. All players need the same version of the modpack. Host a server using the appropriate modded server jar, or use a hosting service that supports your mod loader.
Something seems broken or missing from the world. Is that intentional?
Possibly. The modpack is built around the premise that some content is lost or incomplete. If something seems like a genuine bug rather than an intentional gap, report it on the Modrinth or CurseForge page and we'll take a look.
Where can I find the music discs?
In places you wouldn't expect. Keep exploring.
I found a secret. Where do I talk about it?
The Discord is the best place — keep spoiler content in the appropriate channel so others can discover things for themselves.
Will there be more updates?
Yes, as long as development continues. Follow the modpack on Modrinth for notifications.

Still stuck?

Ask in the community Discord or leave a comment on the Modrinth page.
